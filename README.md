# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes 12 months of e-commerce sales data from the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset. The objective was to identify sales trends, evaluate product category performance, understand customer satisfaction, and build an interactive dashboard for management decision-making.

The project was completed using **Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI** and demonstrates end-to-end data analytics skills including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, dashboard development, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

An e-commerce company wants to understand:

* Which product categories generate the most revenue?
* Which months perform best?
* How customer reviews impact business performance?
* What trends exist in sales and order behavior?
* What actions can improve overall business growth?

The goal is to provide management with actionable insights through a one-page dashboard.

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The following business questions were analyzed:

### 1. Which product category generated the highest revenue?

Revenue was aggregated by product category to identify top-performing categories.

### 2. Which month had peak sales?

Monthly sales trends were analyzed to determine seasonal demand patterns.

### 3. What is the average order value trend?

Average order values were calculated to understand customer spending behavior.

### 4. What is the customer review score distribution?

Customer satisfaction was evaluated using review scores.

### 5. What is the order status distribution?

Order fulfillment performance was assessed across different order statuses.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Insight 1

The Beauty & Health category generated the highest revenue, indicating strong customer demand.

**Recommendation:** Increase promotional spending and inventory allocation for this category.

### Insight 2

Sales performance varies significantly across months, highlighting seasonal purchasing behavior.

**Recommendation:** Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of peak periods.

### Insight 3

Most customer reviews are rated 4 or 5 stars, reflecting high customer satisfaction.

**Recommendation:** Showcase customer reviews and ratings in marketing campaigns.

### Insight 4

A small number of categories contribute a large share of total revenue.

**Recommendation:** Focus growth strategies on top-performing categories while optimizing underperforming ones.

### Insight 5

The majority of orders are successfully delivered.

**Recommendation:** Continue monitoring fulfillment processes and reduce cancellation rates.
